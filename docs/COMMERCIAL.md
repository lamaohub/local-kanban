# Using local-kanban at work

The board is [MIT-licensed](../LICENSE) and stays that way. Every feature is available to everyone,
nothing is switched off, nothing expires, and the board never calls home to check anything — it
listens on `127.0.0.1` and that does not change.

So this page is a request, not a rule.

## The ask

If the board earns you money — you use it inside a company, or on paid client work — **$12 a year
per person** keeps it maintained. That is a dollar a month for something you keep open all day.

Per person means per person who actually uses it: five people on the team, five places. Nothing in
the software counts them and nothing ever will; you say what the number is.

Personal use, studies, hobby projects and non-profits: free, and please keep it that way.

## Getting an invoice

Accounting rarely pays without a document, so ask for one: write to
**thedanielmarkoff@gmail.com** and say

- which country the company is in,
- how many people use the board.

An invoice comes back that your accounting can process. If you would like the company listed as a
supporter in the README, say so and it gets added.

## What this is not

- **Not a licence purchase.** MIT already gives you every right you need, including commercial use.
- **Not a support contract.** No response time is promised and none is owed.
- **Not a subscription.** Nothing switches off, degrades or nags when it lapses, because nothing
  knows it lapsed.

If any of this ever changes, it will be written here, and it will only apply to versions released
after the change.
